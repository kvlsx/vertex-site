Features

What Vertex does

Vertex takes care of the window around your services: the sidebar, notifications and settings. The services themselves work exactly as in the browser.

01

Separate sessions

Each service keeps its cookies and data apart from the others. Add the same messenger three times and stay signed in to three accounts.

02

macOS notifications

New messages arrive as regular macOS notifications. You manage them in System Settings, like for any other app.

03

130 services in the catalog

Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord, Gmail, Notion, Figma, Zoom and many more. Any other website can be added by its address.

04

Light on memory and battery

Services you are not using are slowed down and put to sleep after a few minutes. They wake up as soon as you open them.

05

Ad and tracker blocking

Optional extensions block ads and cookie banners or send services through a proxy. They contain filter lists and settings, never code.

06

Themes and icons

11 colour palettes, each in a light and a dark version, and 13 app icons to choose from.

07

Custom CSS and JavaScript

Change how a service looks or behaves with your own CSS and JavaScript. Export it to a file and import it on another Mac.

08

Your own order

Drag services in the sidebar into the order you want. Vertex keeps it.

Getting started

Installation

Download the DMG, open it and drag Vertex into Applications. On the first launch macOS asks you to confirm once.

First launch: confirm once

Vertex is not yet signed with a paid Apple developer certificate, so macOS stops the first launch with “Vertex Not Opened”. Click Done.

Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Vertex and confirm with your password. From then on Vertex opens like any other app.

If you prefer Terminal, this command does the same in one step:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Vertex.app

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Vertex free?

Yes. There is no paid version, no trial and no subscription.

Why does macOS say “Vertex Not Opened”?

Vertex is not yet signed with a paid Apple developer certificate. Confirm it once under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Where are my logins stored?

On your Mac, separately for each service. When you remove a service, its data is deleted too.

Can I add a website that is not in the catalog?

Yes. Enter its address and it is added as a service with its own session.

Does Vertex run on Intel Macs?

Not yet. Vertex currently needs a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 12 or later.

What does the QR transfer include?

The list of services with names, addresses and notification settings, plus the language and a few options. Logins, cookies and custom code are not transferred.
